# Try new features

A short hands-on checklist for recent changes. Open the Viewer tab with any scan first.

## Side panel

The control boxes sit in a foldable panel on the right of the views.

1. Fold a box: click the title **Hook** (it starts folded) to open it, then click it again. The
   triangle turns and the box body hides and shows; what you set in it is kept.
2. Fold the panel: click the narrow handle at the panel's left edge. The panel shrinks to a thin
   strip and the three views grow to the full width; click the handle again to restore it.
3. Resize the window: drag the window narrower and wider. The views re-fit and the status bar stays
   visible; the panel keeps its width.
4. Scroll: open **Hook**, **Value** and **Control** too and make the window short. A scrollbar appears
   in the panel and the mouse wheel over the panel scrolls it; there is no horizontal scrolling.
